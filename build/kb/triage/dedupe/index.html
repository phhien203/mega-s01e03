Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report is a duplicate only when evidence supports the same cause as an open ticket (decision 0002, superseding decision 0001):

  1. Read its body and look up its tenant’s locale, form version and channel.
  2. Check the cause, not just the normalized subject or intake tag.
  3. Merge into the ticket for that cause; different causes stay separate even with identical subjects.
  4. Deduplicate by report ID. Preserve the body and provenance. Unknown tenants or unclear causes stay unmerged for investigation.
Apply this rule to new arrivals now. #4471 contains separate parser and form-hint problems: use the tickets in the incident review, not one “known” bucket.

When you are unsure

Do not merge an uncertain report. Ask for the original input, tenant configuration, expected and actual result, and any timezone evidence needed to reproduce it. Review live arrivals while the fixes are pending.
